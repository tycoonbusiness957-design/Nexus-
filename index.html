LEGAL

Terms & Privacy

A plain-language summary.

1. What NEXUS is

NEXUS is a planning tool. Calculations, forecasts, simulations and AI answers are estimates based on the numbers you enter and the assumptions shown. They are not financial, legal or tax advice and they are not guaranteed results. You make your own decisions, and the seller is not responsible for business losses from decisions based on these estimates, to the extent the law allows.

2. Your data

Your leads, business numbers, settings and scenarios are saved in your NEXUS account, in a database run by the seller on Supabase, so you can sign in from any device. The seller can technically access that database. Keep your password private. You can also copy a backup in Settings.

3. AI features

When you use an AI feature, the relevant data (for example your leads and numbers) is sent to Claude, made by Anthropic, through the seller's account to produce an answer. Do not use AI features with information you are not comfortable sharing that way.

4. Your customers' information

If you store names and phone numbers of your own customers or leads, you are responsible for having their permission and for following the data protection laws that apply to you, such as the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

5. Plans and payment

Paid plans are bought by messaging the seller on WhatsApp and are activated with a license key. One key is for one customer and should not be shared. A plan lasts for the term you bought and returns to Free when it ends. Your plan is saved on the server and linked to your account. For payment or refund questions, message the seller on WhatsApp.

6. Availability

NEXUS is provided as it is. Features may change, and errors can occur.
